About Bravon Hills

Building reliable business relationships through quality products and professional services.

Bravon Hills Investment CC is a diversified business based in Windhoek, Namibia.

The company operates across several areas, including export, catering, cosmetics and beauty, as well as the supply and delivery of beauty products.

Our focus is on providing dependable services and products while building strong and lasting relationships with our customers and business partners.

We are committed to professional service, reliability and customer satisfaction.
