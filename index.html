About Us

Bravon Hills Investment CC

Bravon Hills Investment CC is a Namibian business based in Windhoek, providing services and products across multiple business sectors.

Our registered principal business activities include export, catering, cosmetics and beauty, as well as the supply and delivery of beauty products.

We are committed to providing reliable products and services while building lasting relationships with our customers and business partners.
